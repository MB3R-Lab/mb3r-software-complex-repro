# Third-party and source notices

The repository includes minimal evidence fixtures derived from configurations associated with the two published study artifacts:

- [a-a-k/socialnet-resilience](https://github.com/a-a-k/socialnet-resilience), MIT License;
- [a-a-k/otel-demo-resilience](https://github.com/a-a-k/otel-demo-resilience), MIT License.

Those study artifacts in turn exercise the DeathStarBench Social Network and OpenTelemetry Demo systems. The fixtures committed here are limited to static configuration, topology, instrumentation, and test-definition fragments required by the Procrustes preflight demonstration; they are not complete distributions of either upstream system.

The published container builds and includes the following MB3R-Lab tools, each under the MIT License:

- [Procrustes](https://github.com/MB3R-Lab/Procrustes);
- [Bering](https://github.com/MB3R-Lab/Bering);
- [Sheaft](https://github.com/MB3R-Lab/Sheaft).

Exact source commits are recorded in `toolchain-lock.json`. Upstream copyright and license texts remain available in their respective source repositories.
